  • Seasoning cube
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Salt
  • Chicken or beef
  • Sweet corn

Steps I Take In Preparing Stir-fry spag

Step 1:
Boil water in a pot, add salt and cook the spaghetti until ut is soft, drain it and set it aside
Step 2:
wash and cut the carrots, green pepper, red pepper, green beans, and onions
Step 3:
Heat some vegetable oil and add the chopped onions
Step 4:
Add the vegetables and stir-fry them for a few minutes until they are tender.
Step 5:
Add the cooked spaghetti to the vegetables and mix everything together
Step 6:
Add the soy sauce, seasoning cube, curry powder, thyme and add salt to taste
Step 7:
Stir everything together and cook for a few more minutes
Step 8:
Serve the stir-fry spaghetti while hot
